### Legend of Evidence Statuses
* **`Verified`**: Directly backed by audited SEC regulatory filings or company-authored engineering publications with specific architectural or quantitative data.
* **`Partially verified`**: Corroborated in general architecture but missing low-level implementation constants or internal parameters.
* **`Interpretation`**: Logical inference derived by synthesizing multiple verified findings with industry standards.
* **`Not publicly established`**: Information deliberately kept proprietary (e.g., detailed monthly AWS line-item invoices, per-tenant unit costs) and explicitly identified as an evidence boundary.
